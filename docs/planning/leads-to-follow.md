# RVS Centenary 2026 — Relevant Initiatives, Links & Contacts

---

## Read this first

A lot of what's below is **other people's material and other people's contact details**, gathered inside a private group for a *specific* purpose (the school's official Centenary souvenir and meet). Before you pull any of it into your website:

- **The photo email is a submission inbox, not a shared library.** `rvcentenaryphotographs@gmail.com` is where alumni *send* photos for the printed souvenir. You can't just "take all their photos" — nobody has published a downloadable archive, and the people running it haven't agreed to hand their collection to a third-party website. The right move is to **contact the coordinator (B.R. Venkatesh) and ask** whether they'd share/cross-post, and to invite people to submit to *your* library directly.
- **The email addresses below were shared for registration/database purposes.** Scraping ~45 personal Gmail/Hotmail addresses into a newsletter list without asking is exactly the kind of thing that gets a project distrusted early. Better to announce your site in the group and let people opt in.
- Treat this as a **map of who's already doing what**, so you can partner with them rather than duplicate or step on toes.

## 1. The big one — official Centenary Souvenir (photos + writing)

This is the most relevant active initiative. The core organising committee is producing a printed **souvenir** plus an **e-book** for everything that doesn't make the print cut. Two submission streams:

**Photos** → `rvcentenaryphotographs@gmail.com`
- Coordinator: **B.R. Venkatesh (ISC 1980)**
- Wants: captioned photos of people, events, activities, celebrations. Proper JPEG files, *not* photos-of-photos.
- Explicitly does **not** want more of the iconic spots (Rishi Konda, Cave Rock, Sliding Rock, Banyan tree, etc.) — they're oversupplied.
- Captions should include names, event description, approximate year.
- Deadlines came and went (15 May → 15 June 2026), so this stream may now be **closed** — worth confirming.

**Stories / articles / poetry** → `RishiValleyCentenary2026@gmail.com`
- Coordinated by **Aruna and Shivanand Reddy**
- Personal RV memories, ≤400 words. Prompt themes offered: connection to nature/first hike, everlasting friendships, favourite teacher or class, K-related teachings (questioning, self-awareness, simplicity), first/last time at RV.
- Original deadline **31 May 2026**.

**Logo & T-shirt designs** → `RishiValleyCentenary2026@gmail.com`
- Coordinated by **Jayanth Tagore**

> This is a near-exact overlap with your site (essays, poems, pictures). The committee has already done the outreach and collection work. **Talk to B.R. Venkatesh and the Reddys first** — a partnership likely gets you a large curated, captioned photo/writing set instead of you rebuilding it.
