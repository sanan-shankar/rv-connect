## The rules it was written to

Two things you've said, which pull in opposite directions:

- **2026-07-25**, on the old library: *"this library sucks, it is an awful library of questions"*,
  *"none of these are cool"*, and *"I think we don't need too many questions in the library. Just a
  few."* That review also left two rules in the code. Every question can be answered in a sentence
  or two (the biography question nobody answered). And "Small things", the one set you kept, is
  the register the rest follow: concrete, recent, low stakes, no sentiment required.
- **2026-09-09**, on a longer library: *"Yes, I need your advice on how to improve that ... without
  making that in library super long."* And: *"we can start creating this by looking at the
  questions that people actually like."*

So the list is longer, but nobody ever sees all of it. A Keeper sees six at a time with a way to
see six more, and a Catch-up never sees a question it has already asked (`features.md` §2).

What your own Edition said people liked (`features.md` §2, hearts per answer): the nosy question
won by a distance, the deep one came second to last, the two one-liners did fine, and the songs
question came last. So each group below is sized by how many an Edition of about eight wants.

Nothing in here asks what the Valley taught you or what you miss. That was the set you cut.

Marks: **[kept]** is already in the library; **[used]** was asked in a live Edition and is worth
keeping; everything unmarked is new.
