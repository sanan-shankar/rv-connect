The work is in `src/app/lab/catchups/_settings.tsx` and the room is `/lab/catchups/settings`; the
session log entry in [`handover.md`](handover.md) carries the numbers. **Five of these rows were
rewritten after round two below, which reversed the answer rather than refining it** -- the first
pass deleted every container in the panel and he found the result less approachable than what it
replaced.

| | Answered by |

| S5, S10 | Cinnamon appears twice in the whole flow: the words "Cannot be undone" where a row's value goes, and the button that does the thing. Three coloured things became one, and the one is language rather than a symbol |
