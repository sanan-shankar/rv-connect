# His notes on the settings surface, in his own words

**Date: 2026-09-09, spoken, transcribed.** Given on the settings list as it stood at
`/lab/catchups/sketches`, behind the Settings door on the picture, at the start of the session that
reworked it (spec 10.3). Where this file and anything earlier disagree, this file wins: it is later
and it is him.

**What was cleaned, and what was not.** The same treatment `brief.md` documents. Fillers ("um",
"uh", "like" as a tic, "you know") are removed; stutters and restarts are merged. Everything else is
his: same content, same order, every hedge, every reversal, every self-correction, because the
register is part of the instruction. Where the transcription is plainly a mis-hearing the word as
transcribed stays in the sentence and the guess sits beside it in square brackets. Paragraph numbers
in **[Sn]** are the only structural addition.

He opened by saying what he was doing: *"As part of the settings rework, I just thought I'd give
some comments about the current settings."*

---

## Where he was coming from

**[S1]** The previous one was very scattered. As shipped right now it was very scattered. It looked
decent, but there were a lot of pills, which is fine. It was not okay. But there's horizontal lines
which I really didn't like, separating lines for no reason. So that's why I didn't like any of that.
And now we've compiled it all into one place. And we made this super functional kind of thing. But
another problem is that this is not really that pretty.

## Walking it

**[S2]** So let's say I open up settings. Firstly, this Edition, this Catch-up font way too small.
It's honestly not that bad. Okay.

**[S3]** When I hover over, the status thing is not that clear. But when I click on it, the [state
layer] kind of extends beyond it. It's not done properly.

**[S4]** Settings are fine. That is weirdly... I can scroll left to right on the settings dialog.
And top to bottom. I feel like some content is getting cut off. I don't know.

**[S5]** There's just something about orange because it's dangerous, but I'm wondering if there's a
better way we can do that. Maybe.

**[S6]** I don't like the brown outlines for those icons, like the icons have that brown background.
I don't like that brown. I think that's probably my main gripe with it. I'm just not... it's just
because something is not pretty about these settings. It seems a bit icky and I don't really want to
read it.

**[S7]** These headings are not nice. Feels like it's a bit cramped. And not something that Apple
would really make.

**[S8]** Also, I would love to see what happens when I click on each one of these because none of
these is clickable right now under the sketches in lab.

**[S9]** On 4.5 [a later look] more separate feedback: it doesn't cut out anymore, which is good.
When I hover over one of them, it darkens and I just can't see that horrible brown colour anymore.
Another problem with the brown.

**[S10]** I don't know if the way to highlight the dangerous ones is to make the icon and the
subtitle orange. Something that doesn't seem right about that. It seems not right with all three.
There's something you missed. Something that's throwing me off about this. I don't know what it is.

**[S11]** On phone also, some of the text wraps. I mean, it doesn't wrap, it [truncates] into the
dots. And that means I literally can't see the description because it's given nowhere else. So maybe
we just have to change the copy. And "End this catch up" as a [row]: you can delete, nothing new
[stays readable]. And leave is pretty self-explanatory. Do I need a description? But I guess we do.
But yeah, usually I [dislike] ellipses.

**[S12]** And "This Catch-up", "You" -- are things really aligned to anything? They're just randomly
hanging there.

## The sheet that comes up from the bottom

**[S13]** Anytime we have this dialogue that pops up from the bottom, I don't want it to be
controlled by that pill, that very thin pill on top. I want it to be controlled by an x. And it
doesn't say Settings. Also, I'd like it to say Settings on the top left and then have the x on the
top right, something like that.

**[S14]** And also I'd like you to be able to bring it down by swiping down on it if you were at the
top. So if you're at the top and you have no more scrolling to do, then when you scroll up or swipe
down, which is scrolling up, then it should just bring the dialogue down.

He closed with: *"These are my thoughts for the settings rework."*
