# Which birds to add, and why

Written 2026-10-08 by the set-up session, from the PDF at `sanan's stuff/The Birds of Rishi
Valley.pdf` (a campus-only list, "soon to be complete") held against the fifty at `/birds`. The
two criteria are the owner's, ¶2 and ¶3 of [`brief.md`](brief.md): colour that works together and
reads against the app's paper, and a bird that is obviously different from every other bird in the
set, "particularly the black ones". The build criterion is ¶4: cuteness, and nothing else.

Everything in this file is **RECOMMENDED**, the set-up session's judgment. The owner decides
replacements (¶39, "just run it by me"); those are questions in the handover, not picks here.

## 1. What the fifty already are

Counted off `e2e/.shots/birds/sheet-96.png` by the colour that fills the disc at avatar size:

| Family | Count | Birds |
|---|---|---|
| Green | 11 | Pitta, both Parakeets, both Bee-eaters, Barbet, Leafbird, Purple-rumped Sunbird, Green-Pigeon, White-eye, Blue-faced Malkoha (olive) |
| Brown, tan, grey, rufous | 15 | Owlet, Hornbill, Sirkeer Malkoha, R-W Bulbul, Starling, Lapwing, Spurfowl, Pond Heron, Treepie, Paradise Flycatcher, Kite, Shrike, Dove, Thrush, Minivet |
| Black and near-black | 10 | Magpie-Robin, Indian Robin, Koel, Drongo, Coucal, Cormorant, Jacobin, Black Eagle, Munia, Peregrine (slate) |
| Blue and teal | 6 | Peafowl, both Kingfishers, Verditer, Tickell's, Roller |
| Yellow and gold | 5 | Y-T Bulbul, both Orioles, Weaver, Flameback |
| One of a kind | 4 | Avadavat (red), Purple Sunbird (violet), Cattle Egret (white), Hoopoe (cinnamon) |

So the set is heavy on green, brown and black, which is where look-alikes breed. What it has
none of: a pale grey-and-white bird that is not a wader, a purple-blue body, slate over lemon,
chestnut under a blue-grey head, a shimmer collar, and three silhouettes every child knows: a
duck, a rooster, an owl with horns. Every pick below fills one of those gaps; nothing below adds
an eleventh green or an eleventh black.

## 2. The twelve to build

For each: the real colours (the drawer draws these, not a palette), the ONE signature that must
survive 40px, the gap it fills, its nearest neighbour in the set and what keeps them apart.

1. **Grey Junglefowl** (Gallus sonneratii), the valley's wild rooster. Grey body finely
   spangled; golden-yellow and orange hackle spots over the neck and mantle; red comb and
   wattles; a long glossy green-black sickle tail. Signature: the comb and the tail curl. Gap: a
   gamebird with a crest, and red-grey-gold together. Neighbour: the Painted Spurfowl (chestnut
   with white moons, no comb); the Peafowl (blue). Cute as a plump hen-shaped body with a jaunty
   comb; the tail is a soft curl, never a spike.
2. **Indian Spot-billed Duck**. Pale fawn-grey body with soft brown scalloped scales; dark
   crown and eye-stripe; a black bill with a bright yellow tip and two red spots at its base; a
   green wing speculum; orange legs. Signature: the duck bill with the yellow tip. Gap: there is
   no duck, and no cream-with-scales. Neighbour: the Pond Heron (buff) and the Lapwing (sand),
   both pointed; a duck is flat-billed and sits low.
3. **Grey-headed Swamphen** (the PDF's Purple Swamphen). A big purple-blue body; pale grey-blue
   head; a huge red bill and frontal shield; red legs; white undertail. Signature: the red shield
   on purple. Gap: cobalt-purple with red. Neighbour: the Purple Sunbird (a small violet jewel
   with a dark curved bill); the swamphen is a chunky hen with a comically big red nose.
4. **Black-winged Kite**. Pearl-grey back; white head and underparts; jet-black shoulders; ruby
   eyes under a thin black brow; a small black hooked bill. Signature: red eye and black shoulder
   on white. Gap: a pale raptor, grey and white. Neighbour: the Cattle Egret (white wader, yellow
   dagger); the Brahminy Kite (chestnut). Cute as a soft, dove-shaped ghost with ruby eyes.
5. **Indian Eagle-Owl** (the PDF's Rock Eagle-Owl). Tawny-brown mottled body; a paler buff face;
   two upright ear tufts; big orange eyes; grey bill. Signature: tufts and orange eyes. Gap: a
   horned owl (the Owlet is round-headed with yellow eyes). Neighbour: the Spotted Owlet; keep
   this body warmer and redder, the eyes orange, and let the tufts break the circle.
6. **Common Tailorbird**. Tiny. Olive-green back and wings; whitish-cream underparts; a rust
   cap; a long thin tail cocked straight up; pale pink legs. Signature: the cocked-up tail and
   the rust cap. Gap: a tiny upright bird in olive, cream and rust. Neighbour: the Green Bee-eater
   (emerald, horizontal, turquoise throat) and the White-eye (olive ball); keep the body pale and
   the tail vertical.
7. **Common Kestrel**, the male. Chestnut back spotted black; blue-grey head and tail with a
   black tail band; cream breast finely streaked; a dark tear-drop moustache; yellow cere and
   feet. Signature: a blue-grey head on a chestnut back, black band on the tail. Gap: a warm
   raptor. Neighbour: the Peregrine (slate and white, hooded) and the Brahminy Kite (chestnut
   with a WHITE head); the kestrel's head is grey and its back is spotted.
8. **Black-crowned Night Heron**. Stocky and hunched. Black cap and back; pale grey wings; white
   face and underparts; big red eyes; stout black bill; short yellow legs. Signature: red eye
   under a black cap, hunched. Gap: a cool-toned stocky heron. Neighbour: the Pond Heron (buff,
   streaky) and the Cormorant (all dark, snake neck).
9. **Pied Kingfisher**. White with black speckling and bars; a shaggy black-and-white crest; a
   broad black breast band; a long black dagger bill; white brow. Signature: crest, dagger,
   breast band. Gap: the most-watched hoverer over any tank, and a white bird that is not a
   wader. **Risk**: black-and-white, beside the Magpie-Robin, the Jacobin and the Munia; the
   reviewer's 40px check is the gate, and if it cannot be made distinct it moves to the reserve.
10. **Wire-tailed Swallow**. Glossy steel-blue upperparts; pure white underparts; a chestnut
    cap; two long wire-thin tail streamers (drawn as two slender soft ribbons, never needles);
    a tiny black bill. Signature: blue over white with a rust cap, swept. Gap: blue-and-white.
    Neighbour: Tickell's (cobalt with an orange throat) and the Verditer (aqua all over).
11. **Grey Wagtail**. Slate-grey back; lemon-yellow belly and vent; white brow; black throat
    (the breeding male); a very long black-and-white tail; pink legs. Signature: lemon under
    slate, a long tail. Gap: slim grey-and-lemon. Neighbour: the Yellow-throated Bulbul, which
    is grey with a yellow HEAD; the wagtail's yellow is low and its tail is long.
12. **Rock Pigeon**, the campus pigeon. Blue-grey body, darker head; an iridescent green-and-
    purple neck patch; two black wing bars; white rump; coral-red feet; a small grey bill with a
    white cere. Signature: the shimmer collar, the two bars, the puffed chest. Gap: grey with
    iridescence, and everyone's bird. Neighbour: the Hornbill (grey ball with a banana bill, no
    bars) and the Laughing Dove (rose-fawn).

## 3. The reserve, built if the twelve clear the bar and time remains

- **Spot-breasted Fantail**: dark grey-brown; white brow; white-spotted breast; a tail fanned
  wide. A silhouette nothing else has; dark, so it goes last.
- **Scaly-breasted Munia**: chocolate head and back; cream belly scalloped in dark scales; a
  stout dark bill. Spots on cream, the inverse of the Spurfowl.
- **Black-headed Ibis**: white body; bare black head and neck; a long down-curved black bill.
- **Little Grebe**: a dark brown fluff ball with chestnut cheeks and a pale spot at the gape, no
  tail to speak of; the roundest bird on the list.
- **Common Iora**: yellow with black wings and two white wing bars. Only if the reviewer can hold
  it apart from the two orioles at 40px.
- **Blue-capped Rock-Thrush**: blue cap and throat, orange underparts, black mask, white wing
  patch. Only if Tickell's Blue Flycatcher is replaced (handover question 4); together they clash.

## 4. Considered and not taken

- **Red-vented Bulbul**: the commonest bird in the school, and a dark head over a red vent is
  the Indian Robin's and the Red-whiskered Bulbul's read at 40px. Question 6 asks anyway.
- **Spotted Dove**: the chessboard nape is lovely; the bird is the Laughing Dove's shape and
  colour.
- **White-browed Bulbul**, the PDF's "Murukku bird": olive-grey with a yellow vent and no mark
  that survives 40px. School lore against colour; question 6.
- **Jungle Babbler** and the six other babblers: grey-brown, at best a yellow eye.
- **House Crow, Large-billed Crow**, the four other **drongos**, **Pied Bushchat**, **Coot**,
  **Moorhen**, **Glossy Ibis**: dark birds, the family ¶3 names as the one that blurs.
- **Shikra, Black Kite, Bonelli's, Tawny, Spotted** and **Short-toed Snake-Eagle**,
  **Honey-buzzard**, **White-eyed Buzzard**: brown raptors that land on the Black Eagle. The
  Kestrel and the Black-winged Kite are the two raptors on the list that look like nothing else.
- **Long-tailed Shrike, Brown Shrike, Common Woodshrike**: the Bay-backed Shrike's mask.
- **Blue-tailed Bee-eater** (a third bee-eater), **White-cheeked Barbet** (a second green
  barbet), **Loten's Sunbird** (a third sunbird), **Black-naped Oriole** (a third oriole),
  **White-naped Woodpecker** (a second flameback), **Indian Cormorant**, **Red Spurfowl**,
  **Eurasian Collared Dove**: each a paler copy of a bird already drawn.
- **Yellow-footed Green-Pigeon** and **Red-wattled Lapwing**: the PDF's versions of two birds
  already in the set. Swaps, so questions 2 and 3 in the handover, not picks.
- **Grey Heron**: tall and grey; the Night Heron is the heron that brings a colour.
- **Mottled Wood-Owl, Brown Fish-Owl, Indian Scops Owl**: one tufted owl is enough, and the
  Eagle-Owl's orange eyes are the strongest second-owl mark.
- **Jerdon's** and **Indian Nightjar**: camouflage by design.
- **Grey Francolin, Jungle Bush-Quail, Barred Buttonquail**: round brown gamebirds; the
  Junglefowl is the gamebird.
- Every **lark, pipit, prinia, cisticola, warbler, flowerpecker, swift**, the **Asian Brown**
  and **Taiga Flycatchers**: small brown or grey birds with no mark that survives 40px.
- **Barn** and **Red-rumped Swallow**: the Wire-tailed is the swallow with the cleanest three
  colours.
- **White-browed Wagtail**: black-and-white; the Grey Wagtail adds the lemon.
- **Indian Silverbill, White-rumped Munia**: pale munias; the Scaly-breasted is the reserve one.
- **Blue Rock-Thrush**: dull blue all over; the Verditer owns "all blue".
- **Common Hawk-Cuckoo, Grey-bellied Cuckoo**: grey.
