# U2b, independent review 2 (2026-10-15)

Review 2 of U2b: verification only, after the drawer's one fix pass on the nineteen faults of [review 1](u2b-review-1.md). For each bird: is each review-1 fault fixed, and did the fix create a new checklist fault. Judged against `reviewer.md` by the same Opus reviewer, who did not draw these birds.

Four pass: 14 Magpie-Robin, 29 Golden Oriole, 38 Common Kingfisher, 39 Jerdon's Leafbird. Three fail: 34 Blue-faced Malkoha (1 new fault), 48 Green-Pigeon (1 new fault), 51 Yellow-footed Green-Pigeon (1 fault not fixed, 1 new). Of the nineteen, eighteen are fixed. The one that is not is 51's neck notches. The three new faults all come from one move: lifting a head out of the body left a colour patch on that head with a rim of under 1.5 units between it and the paper. Coordinates are viewBox units (0..100) from the source. "px" is a point on that bird's 600px render.

Runner's scope decisions, applied and not failed: the Oriole's head stays inside the body (he flagged only the sunglasses), and the Leafbird's eye is a brown iris with an ink pupil inside the mask.
