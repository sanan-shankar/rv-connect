## Findings

### L10-01 — An unconfirmed or unvouched account gets inside Catch-ups three ways the trust model never checks: its claimed batch at signup, a Keeper's picker, and the Keeper's hat. Inside, it reads the names, answers and photographs the directory withholds, and with the hat it can nudge, publish, remove and end with no stage gate
- Severity: Medium
- Confidence: certain on the mechanism. Every line below was read, and live rows show two of the three doors already in use. What would change my mind: an owner ruling that reading a Catch-up is a Stage 0 capability. The code's own invite-link comment rules the other way (Stage 2).
- Where:
  - The rule as the code states it: `src/app/(main)/catchups/actions.ts:611-614` (`joinCatchupByToken`: *"Joining puts this account inside a private group, reading and writing among people who shared the link in good faith (trust model, Stage 2)."* then `requireVerifiedMember()`). `docs/SECURITY.md:43-50` (Stage 0 sees no names; "EVERY community write (post, comment, upload, report, Catch-ups…)" is Stage 2 only). `src/components/feed/rail/directory-module.tsx:17-25` (names and batches are "directory data" that "holds the directory's own Stage 1 line … a capability lives everywhere its data is serialized"). `src/lib/api-gate.ts:82-93` (names by query are Stage 1).
  - Door 1, the batch claim at signup: `src/components/auth/actions.ts:190-205` calls `joinBatchGroup(user.id, batchYear)` for every new alumnus, before any confirmation. `src/lib/batch-catchups.ts:83-118` has no stage check. At `:146-160` `_count.members >= BATCH_CATCHUP_FLOOR` counts unconfirmed accounts toward the ten that mint the batch's Catch-up. At `:218-250` the nightly heal re-adds any alumnus with a batch year.
  - Door 1b, the batch claim edited later: `src/components/profile/profile-actions.ts:160-202` (the `batchYear` write needs only `auth()`).
