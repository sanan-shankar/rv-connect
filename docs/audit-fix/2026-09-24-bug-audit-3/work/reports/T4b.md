### T4b-01 — The viewer silently switches to a different photograph when the river changes under it, and one everyday edit makes that certain
- Severity: High
- Confidence: certain (the mechanism is read end to end in both files; the edit-year path is deterministic, the band-arrival path is a race with a window of one round trip). What would change my mind: nothing in `ImageViewer` re-finds the photograph by identity, and I looked for it.
- Where: `src/components/common/image-viewer.tsx:267` (`const [index, setIndex] = useState(initialIndex);`), `:339-341` (`const count = images.length; const at = Math.min(Math.max(index, 0), Math.max(count - 1, 0)); const current = images[at];`), `:805` (`key={at}`), `:833` (`src={shown}`); the prop rebuilt under it by `src/components/collection/collection-client.tsx:1316-1320` (`viewerList`/`viewerImages`, memoised on `photos`); the three writers that reorder `photos` while the viewer is open: `:523` (`needBand`: `setPhotos(boxesRef.current.flatMap((b) => held.current.get(b.key) ?? []));`), `:240-251` (the seed guard: `setPhotos(firstPage.photos);`); the deterministic trigger: `src/app/(main)/collection/actions.ts:1262-1263` (`editPhoto` → `revalidatePath("/collection"); revalidatePath(`/collection/${input.id}`);`) with `collection-client.tsx:1751-1754` (`onSaved` → `patch(...)`, which changes the edited row's `photoYear` inside `photos` and `held`).
- Taxonomy: 6b (state keyed by position across an out-of-order update), 6c(3) (a newer screen clobbered by an older index), 6a
- Expected: `ImageViewer`'s own contract, `:386` "Fresh session each open: land on the pressed image"; a viewer stays on the photograph that was pressed until the reader steps.
- Actual, deterministic: in Chronological (the Class Collection's default order, `defaultOrderFor("class")`), open a 1978 photograph, press the pencil, change the year to 1979, Save. "Saved." shows, the dialog closes, and the photograph behind it is now a different one -- and the pencil now edits THAT one. Sequence: `editPhoto` revalidates → the route re-renders → `firstPage.shapes` has one entry moved from 1978's run to 1979's → the `lastIndex` effect sees `sameIndex` false, finds neither 1978 nor 1979 `holdsBand` any more, deletes both from `held` and rebuilds `photos` without them → `viewerImages[at]` is whatever sat `|1978| + |1979|` places further down. Both years are reserved boxes behind the open viewer and still intersect the viewport (the scroll lock is `overflow: hidden` on `<html>`, `image-viewer.tsx:427-429`; observers keep observing), so `needBand` refetches them, `:523` rebuilds again, and `at` now lands one photograph earlier in 1978 than the one edited. Because the frame is keyed by `at` (`:805`) nothing even dissolves: the `<img src>` swaps in place, the placeholder and spinner come back until the new file lands. A caption-only edit is safe (`sameIndex` true); a year change, or a delete/contribution by anybody since the page opened, is not.
- Actual, racy, every day: press a year on the rail, tap a photograph in the year that just drew while its neighbour above is still in flight -- `PhotoRiver`'s observer (`photo-river.tsx:588-602`, `rootMargin: "1200px 0px"`) asks for every reserved year within 1,200px at once, above and below -- and when the year above lands, `:523` rebuilds `photos` in band order and the open photograph is replaced by one from that year. The window is one band round trip, hundreds of ms from a phone to the Mumbai database. In Newest order the seed guard (`:240-251`) replaces `photos` with the server's page one whenever a re-render arrives with a photograph the river lacks: the reader's own edit or delete, after anyone else's valley photograph was approved since the page opened, shifts every index by the number of newcomers.
- Why: `ImageViewer` holds an index and nothing else; `ViewerImage` carries no identity the viewer re-finds (`href` and `src` exist and are not used for this). The Collection is the one caller whose `images` changes while open (a post's handful is fixed), and it changes in three places.
- Proof: throwaway account, its own photograph, Chronological order: open, Edit, change the year by one, Save, watch the dialog's `img[src]` change with no press. Read-only variant: chrome-devtools `emulate` a slow network, press a year on the rail, tap the first photograph that draws, watch the `src`.
- Fix direction: track the photograph, not the slot. Either the viewer re-finds `current` by identity when `images` changes (an `id` on `ViewerImage`, defaulting to `src`; on an `images` change `setIndex(images.findIndex((i) => i.id === currentId))` when found, clamp or close when not), or `CollectionClient` keeps `viewer` as `{ list, id }` and derives the index each render. The former fixes every caller at once. Either way `key={at}` becomes `key={current.id}` so a legitimate re-index does not restart the dissolve.
- Gate: a `node --test` on a pure "index after the list changed" helper (old list, new list, current id → new index or null); an invariant 5 in `e2e/collection-journeys.spec.ts`: after opening a photograph, `dialog img[src]` is unchanged across a forced band arrival (the edit-year path, on the throwaway account's own photograph).
- Known-related: none in the baseline. C-179 is the same family (an older answer stitched into a newer list) for the river; this is the viewer.
