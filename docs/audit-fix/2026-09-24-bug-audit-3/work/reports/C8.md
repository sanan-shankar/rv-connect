Agent: C8 · Started: 2026-10-24 · Model: Opus 5.5 (1M)
Charter: for every kind of thing a member can make, and every way its parent can go away or change
(author delete, admin hide/remove, account deleted / purged / merged / blocked / unverified, Catch-up

cells. New defects only. HEAD read: 36ed27d3 (at start; the audit base was 495ecdd2, 118 commits
earlier; every line below is HEAD's).

## Findings

### C8-01 — An erased or replaced photograph stays publicly viewable through the site's own image address for up to a year: every deletion path deletes the R2 object and none purges the optimizer's copies
- Severity: Medium (the privacy page's erasure promise and the M11 rationale for moderation both fail for every photograph that went through the optimizer: feed and letter photos, avatars, Catch-up answer photos and uploaded Catch-up pictures)
- Confidence: likely. The mechanism is documented by both vendors (quoted below) and nothing in the repo purges; not live-proven, because proving it needs a deletion. What would change my mind: Vercel evicting these entries from its CDN long before the TTL, which its page does not say.
- Where:
  - The upstream lifetime every stored object carries: `src/lib/storage.ts:131, :184, :218` (`CacheControl: "public, max-age=31536000, immutable"`), with the reasoning at `:130` ("Images are content-addressed (cuid filenames), so cache forever") — true of CHANGE, false of DELETION.
  - No `minimumCacheTTL`, no loader, no purge: `next.config.ts:382-395`.
  - What routes through `/_next/image`: `src/lib/image-cdn.ts:56-74` (`/_next/image?url=…&w=750|1080|1456|1920&q=75`, a fixed, guessable ladder) used by `posts/post-card.tsx:611-666` and `letters/letter-images.tsx:42-43`; `next/image` without `unoptimized` in `common/bird-avatar.tsx:115-122` (every avatar, fixed `width={px}`), `catchups/edition/reader-parts.tsx`, `catchups/edition/photo-run.tsx`, `catchups/home/home-head.tsx`, `catchups/index/catchup-card.tsx`, `catchups/index/edition-cover-card.tsx`, `profile/letterhead-profile.tsx`.
  - The deleters, none of which can reach a cached transformation: `feed/actions.ts:72-119` (post/letter delete), `:740-770` (draft edit drops a photo), `account-purge.ts:322-418` + `:428-472` (erasure), `components/settings/actions.ts:136, 140, 163` (avatar replace/remove), `admin/people/actions.ts:428-432` (merge), `catchups/actions.ts:1625-1628` (recording; audio is not optimized, so out of scope), `image-purge.ts:53-69`.
  - `/_next/image` is outside the proxy matcher and needs no session: `src/proxy.ts:422-424` (C5-01 relies on the same fact).
- Taxonomy: 6g (delete paths vs every copy of the bytes) · 6c (a cache layer no invalidation reaches) · privacy promise
- Expected: `privacy/page.tsx:164-166` "After 60 days the account, your posts, comments, photographs and profile are permanently erased, including the stored image files"; the M11 comment at `collection/actions.ts:1164-1172` ("a hidden photo's file stayed at a public, permanent R2 URL that anyone who had noted it could still fetch forever, so 'removed' did not remove the thing that mattered").
- Actual: a member deletes a feed post with a photograph, replaces their avatar, or has their account erased. The R2 object goes (`images.rishivalley.space/...` answers 404). But `https://<site>/_next/image?url=<that address>&w=750&q=75` (and 1080, 1456, 1920, and the avatar's 1x/2x widths) keeps answering 200 with the photograph to anyone, signed in or not, until the cache entry expires. Its lifetime is the upstream `max-age`, one year. A redeploy does not clear it. Anyone who copied an image address from a page (or the page's own `/_next/image` src) before the deletion can keep fetching it.
- Why: Next 16's own documentation for this version: "The expiration … of the optimized image is defined by either the `minimumCacheTTL` or the upstream image `Cache-Control` header, whichever is larger … There is no mechanism to invalidate the cache at this time" (`node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/02-components/image.md:798-802`). Vercel's page (vercel.com/docs/image-optimization, last updated 2026-08-13, read 2026-10-24) for remote images: "TTL is determined by the Cache-Control max-age header from the upstream image or minimumCacheTTL … whichever is larger"; "Redeploying your app doesn't invalidate the image cache"; "You can manually purge or programmatically purge to invalidate all cached transformations of a source image without redeploying". The code sets the longest possible upstream TTL and never calls the purge. Live, read-only: Cloudflare is NOT holding copies (both a live and a purged thumbnail answer `cf-cache-status: DYNAMIC`; the purged ones 404 at the origin), so the optimizer is the one surviving copy that is ours.
- Proof: with the throwaway account at close-out: upload an avatar, load any page that renders it (this fills the cache), note the `/_next/image` src, replace the avatar, then `curl -sI` that src on production: 200 with `x-vercel-cache: HIT` while the raw R2 address is 404. No member data involved.
- Fix direction: either (a) give stored objects a short shared TTL so the optimizer's copy ages out quickly (`Cache-Control: public, max-age=<hours>, s-maxage=<hours>` on upload, plus a backfill of the header on existing objects, and set `images.minimumCacheTTL` low; costs some re-transformations), or (b) call Vercel's programmatic purge-by-source-image from the one place bytes are deleted (`purgeImageUrls` and `drainPendingImagePurges`), best-effort and queued like the R2 delete. (b) keeps today's cost profile; (a) needs no Vercel token. The owner chooses; either way the privacy sentence should say how long a copy can linger.
- Gate: a rule test that `storage.ts`'s `CacheControl` is not `immutable`/one-year, or that `purgeImageUrls`/`drainPendingImagePurges` call the purge helper.
- Known-related: C5-01 (same endpoint, different defect: cost amplification by query string; its `search: ""` fix does not touch this), L11-02 (backups outlive erasure; this is a live, public copy, not a private backup), M11/C-069 (the moderation half of the same promise), C8-02.

### C8-02 — An admin's takedown of a post leaves its photographs at their public addresses for good (three live today)
- Severity: Medium (moderation that removes the words and not the picture; the case that matters most, a photograph posted without someone's consent, is the one it does not handle)
- Confidence: certain (live)
- Where: `src/app/(main)/feed/actions.ts:556-583` (`adminRemovePost`: `post.update({ data: { isHidden: true } })`, bells cleared, nothing about `images`); `src/app/(main)/admin/reports/actions.ts:22-40` (`adminHidePost`, the report queue's takedown: the same); compare `src/app/(main)/collection/actions.ts:1164-1197` (`adminRemovePhoto`: keeps the row hidden AND `purgeImageUrls(photoStoredUrls(photo), "moderation")`, with the M11/C-069 argument in its docblock), and `feed/actions.ts:526-546` (the author's own delete purges). There is no unhide writer for posts anywhere (grep `isHidden: false` writes: none), so the bytes have no future reader but the author's own view of a hidden post.
- Taxonomy: 6g · write-path invariant 4 (bytes outlive the act that retired them) · moderation consistency
- Expected: the rule the Collection was given for the same act (M11, C-069: "'removed by a moderator' has to mean it"), and the `adminRemovePhoto` docblock's own premise that a hidden text post is harmless "once hidden" — which stops being true the moment a post carries a photograph.
- Actual: an admin hides a post or letter (directly or from a report). Members stop seeing the card, but every photograph on it stays at `https://images.rishivalley.space/uploads/...`, fetchable by anybody holding the address (every member who scrolled past it has it in their page HTML), plus the optimizer's copies (C8-01). Live, read-only (2026-10-24): 9 hidden posts, 3 with photographs (`cmropl3h5000004jqu7toubh2`, `cmr97a1mw000004jldgi9zgk2` hidden since 2026-07-18, `cmseet3tq000104ifelo7mskz`); `curl -sI` on each of their three image addresses answers `HTTP/2 200 image/webp` (7,194, 278,222 and 88,438 bytes).
- Why: the M11 fix was written against `adminRemovePhoto` only; the two post takedowns predate it and were never revisited. The bell cleanup (C-054) was later added to both, which is why they look finished.
- Proof: done, read-only: `SELECT id, images FROM "Post" WHERE "isHidden"` then `curl -sI` each URL.
- Fix direction: the Collection's shape: in both takedowns, after the hide commits, `purgeImageUrls(parseJsonArray(post.images), "moderation")` (with deletePostWithImages' "still named by another post" check, since `images` is caller-supplied JSON, C-018), and decide what the author's view of a hidden post shows in place of the photographs (the Collection already accepts a broken frame for the uploader, T7a-20). Purge the three live ones by hand after the owner looks at them. Optionally null `images` on the row so nothing renders a dead address.
- Gate: extend `notification-reach.test.mjs`'s takedown discovery (L9-08) with a second assertion: every `isHidden: true` write on `Post` or `Photo` is followed by `purgeImageUrls`.
- Known-related: T7a-20 (the photo half's broken thumbnails), L9-08 (the same takedown list for bells), C8-01 (the optimizer copy), C8-03 (Catch-up answers have no takedown at all).

### C8-03 — Nothing can take down a Catch-up answer, its photographs or its recording except the author: no admin hide, no report, and blocking leaves it up, so an abusive answer in a published Edition can only be removed by erasing its author's whole account
- Severity: Medium (a moderation hole on the surface most members use; the available lever destroys everything else the person ever wrote)
- Confidence: certain
- Where:
  - `prisma/schema.prisma:1236-1270` (`CatchupEntry`: no `isHidden`, unlike `Post.isHidden` `:718`, `Comment.isHidden` `:777`, `Photo.isHidden` `:449`).
  - Every writer of `CatchupEntry` in `src` (grep `catchupEntry.(update|delete)`): `catchups/actions.ts:1567, 1575, 1631` (the author's own `submitEntry`, only while `answering`, `:1462-1464`) and `admin/people/actions.ts:373-376` (the merge). No admin or Keeper action touches an answer.
  - `src/app/(main)/admin/catchups/[catchupId]/page.tsx` renders answers read-only; the directory has no `actions.ts`.
  - Reports cover posts and people only: `src/components/posts/report-action.ts:178` (`reportPost`) and `:278` (`reportUser`); `Report.targetType` "post" | "user" (`schema.prisma:830`).
  - Blocking hides posts, letters and comments but not answers (T2b-05).
  - The rework assumed otherwise: `docs/planning/catchups-rework/handover.md:2010-2013` (owner question 52: "you cannot check a reported answer in a capsule for a year").
- Taxonomy: 6h identity states (the abusive author) · admin-hides-it column of this round's matrix · write-path symmetry
- Expected: the takedown every other member-made thing has (post: hide; comment: hide; Collection photograph: remove + purge), and a report path for the content, not just for the person.
- Actual: a member posts a cruel answer, or a photograph of somebody who did not consent, or a recording, into a published Edition. Other members can report the PERSON. The admin can read it in the reading room and can block the author, which leaves the answer up (T2b-05). The Keeper cannot touch it. The author is the only person who can remove it, and only while the Edition is still answering. After publication the remaining options are `adminDeleteUser` (erases the account, every post, letter, photograph and answer they ever made) or hand-written SQL, which also leaves the photographs' bytes in R2.
- Why: answers were built as private writing inside a group the members chose, and moderation was left to the feed's tools. Batch Catch-ups (enrolled automatically, about forty people, L10-01) and Keeper-made groups changed who reads them.
- Proof: argue-only (the absence is the proof: the greps above).
- Fix direction: owner decision on the shape. The minimum that matches the rest of the app: `CatchupEntry.isHidden` (dated migration), an admin "Remove answer" in the reading room that hides the row and purges its `images`/`audioUrl` with the existing `purgeImageUrls` (the C8-02/M11 shape), the reader's loader filtering `isHidden` (`catchups-edition-view.ts:118-148`, alongside whatever T2b-05 decides), and a "Report this answer" entry into the existing report flow (`targetType: "entry"`, `entryId`). Mind the sealed-capsule rule (handover Q52): an admin may need to see a reported capsule answer.
- Gate: `gate-coverage.test.mjs`-style assertion that every member-content table with a reader has an admin takedown writer.
- Known-related: T2b-05 (what blocking hides in the reader; this is the missing lever behind it), L10-01 (who ends up inside batch Catch-ups), C8-04 (after a removal nobody, not even the author, can withdraw).

### C8-04 — A member removed by a Keeper, or moved out of a batch by a year correction, mid-Edition still has their unpublished answers and questions published to that group afterwards, and can no longer withdraw them
- Severity: Low (0 live: no answer or question today belongs to someone outside the group; 1 Edition is answering now. Medium if the owner means removal as a moderation tool)
- Confidence: certain
- Where:
  - The three exits, none of which looks at the leaver's rows in a live Edition: `removeCatchupMember` (`src/app/(main)/catchups/actions.ts:2179-2213`: succession, `groupMember.deleteMany`, bells), `leaveCatchup` (`:2242-2299`), `syncBatchGroup` (`src/lib/batch-catchups.ts:230-251`, run on every batch-year edit, `profile-actions.ts:253`, `admin/people/actions.ts:193`, and nightly by the heal `:301-341`).
  - The withdrawal door closes behind them: `submitEntry` → `loadMemberEdition` (`catchups/actions.ts:371-377`, "You are not a member of this group.").
  - The reader does not filter by membership: `src/lib/catchups-edition-view.ts:100-148` (every accepted prompt, every entry, by `editionId`).
  - The promises: `docs/spec/catchups.md:270-271` ("**What they published stays** in the Editions other people have read") and `:280` ("Removing … does the same"); `batch-catchups.ts:224-226` ("their words, if they wrote any, stay in the Editions other people have read"); `leaveCatchup`'s docblock `catchups/actions.ts:2229-2232` ("A published Edition is a keepsake the whole group has read").
- Taxonomy: 6h identity states (member removed / leaves / batch moves) · comment and spec vs code
- Expected: the stated rule covers what was PUBLISHED and already read. An Edition still collecting or answering has been read by nobody, so the "keepsake" reasoning does not reach it.
- Actual: three cases. (1) A Keeper removes someone while answering is open, perhaps because of what they were writing. That person's answers, photographs and recordings, and any question they asked while collecting, go out to the whole group when the Edition publishes. The removed member never sees them published, never gets the bell, and cannot withdraw them, because the only withdrawal is their own `submitEntry`, which now refuses them. With C8-03 nobody else can remove them either. (2) A member corrects their batch year while their old (wrong) batch is answering. Their answers publish into a class they do not belong to, and the correction cannot be followed by a withdrawal. (3) Voluntary leaving does the same, but its confirm copy does say "What you wrote stays" (`settings-surface.tsx:701`), so that case is disclosed. For a time capsule the answers open a year later to the group the member left.
- Why: leaving and removing were designed around published Editions (the 2026-08-21 decision); the in-flight Edition was not considered, and the reader trusts that only members wrote rows.
- Proof: read-only today: `SELECT ... FROM "CatchupEntry" e JOIN "CatchupEdition" ... WHERE NOT EXISTS (member)` grouped by Edition status = 0 rows (and 0 for prompts). Live demo is a write (needs two accounts in one Catch-up).
