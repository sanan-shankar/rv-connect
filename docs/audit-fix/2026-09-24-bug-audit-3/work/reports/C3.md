### C3-01 — "Close and send it out" on an Edition nobody has answered yet does not send it out: it quietly resets the answer deadline to three days from now (often cutting the week short), rings "answers are open" again, spends the Edition's one safety-net extension, and tells the presser nothing; pressing it a second time publishes an empty Edition to everyone

- Confidence: certain on the mechanism (read at source, every line below). What would change my mind: a client surface that reads `extended`/`message` off the result (there is none: `closeAndPublish` has one caller, the settings surface, and it reads only `error`).

- Taxonomy: state-machine hole (a rule written for the deadline applied to a hand press days early) · 6a error/result swallowed · L9 (the message and the confirm copy both describe something that did not happen) · 6i notification fan-out
- Expected: the confirm copy's promise ("the Edition comes out"), or, if the owner wants the zero-answer safety net to hold for a hand press too, a refusal that says so ("Nobody has answered yet") and leaves the deadline and the extension alone. The returned sentence itself promises an EXTENSION ("extended by 3 days"), which for a press made on the first day of seven is a CUT of three days (measured below).
- Actual, in the member's terms (people Catch-up: its Keeper; batch Catch-up since daec81ec: ANY member, stage-ungated per V-T2-01): answering opens on Monday 07:00 IST with a week to write. On Monday evening, before anybody has answered, a member opens Settings, presses "Close and send it out", confirms "Send it out". The dialog closes and the page redraws still in answering, now reading "Answers close Friday" instead of the following Monday: the window lost three days. Everyone taking part (askers, members who opted into reminders) gets a second "Answers are open for X's Catch-up" bell. The member is shown no sentence at all. If they press again (the natural reaction to a button that "did nothing"), the extension bit is now spent, `shouldExtendForTooFew` is false, and the Edition publishes with zero answers: every member gets "Your X Catch-up is ready to read", linking to an empty Edition that stays in every member's archive for good (no code path deletes an Edition; grep `catchupEdition.delete*` finds only the demo seed). And if they do NOT press again, the group now has three days instead of a week, and when those three days end with still nothing written the clock no longer extends (bit spent) and publishes the empty Edition itself.
- Why: `closeAndPublish` borrowed the clock's too-few branch verbatim ("Evaluates the too-few-answers rule (spec 2.6) exactly like the natural close", docblock `:1081-1091`), but the clock only reaches that branch AT the deadline, while the Keeper's (now anyone's) early press reaches it at any point in the week. `extendPatch` anchors on `now`, unlike `extendPhasePatch` (`catchups-core.ts:806-820`), which anchors on `max(deadline, now)` precisely so that "extend" can never move a live deadline earlier (its own docblock, audit C-028). The surface then discards the only signal that something other than a publish happened.
- Proof: run (pure functions, no database), a /tmp script importing `src/lib/catchups-core.ts` the way `catchups-core.test.mjs` does: answering opened Mon 2026-10-19 01:30 UTC (07:00 IST) gives `answersCloseAt` 2026-10-26T01:30Z; a press at 2026-10-19T13:00Z with 0 answers: `shouldExtendForTooFew` = true, `extendPatch(...).answersCloseAt` = 2026-10-23T01:30Z (**3 days lost**); with that patch applied, `shouldExtendForTooFew` = false, i.e. the second press publishes. A press in roughly the first four days of the week moves the deadline EARLIER; one in the last three moves it later; either way the safety net is spent. Live, with the throwaway account in a people Catch-up it keeps (NEVER on Batch of 2024's running Edition, which has 52 answers and would not take this branch anyway): start an Edition, ask one question, Open answering, press Close and send it out with no answer written; the home's "Answers close" date moves to three days out and no toast appears; press again; the Edition publishes empty.
- Fix direction: decide the rule for a hand press with zero answers (owner): either refuse it with a sentence ("Nobody has answered yet; give it time or extend instead") without touching `remindersSent` or `answersCloseAt`, or publish as the copy promises. If the extension is kept, anchor it on `max(answersCloseAt, now)` like `extendPhasePatch`, pass `excludeUserId`, and make the settings surface show the action's `message` when `extended` is true. Either way the confirm copy and the returned sentence must describe the same outcome.
- Gate: a unit test on the pure half (an early-press plan never produces an `answersCloseAt` earlier than the stored one), plus a source pin that the settings surface surfaces a non-error `message` from `closeAndPublish`.
- Known-related: V-T2-01 / L10-01 (who may press it; this is what the press does, for anyone); T3-06 (the same surface's other optimistic-result gap, on "Give everyone longer"); T2b-12 (a legacy empty row makes `entryCount` non-zero and skips this branch).
