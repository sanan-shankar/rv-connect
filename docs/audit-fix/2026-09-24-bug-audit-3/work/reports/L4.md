### L4-07 — `reportSwallowed` never flushes, so a report raised at the end of an invocation — the nightly tick, the sweep, the tail of an `after()` — may never leave the instance
- Severity: Low (the reports it can lose are the unattended ones: the engine's nightly pass and the retention sweep, where nobody is watching and nothing else will say it)

  - `node_modules/@sentry/core/build/cjs/utils/flushIfServerless.js` — the SDK ships a `flushIfServerless` for exactly this, and nothing in the repo or in `@sentry/nextjs` calls it for a manual capture
  - the callers most exposed: `src/app/api/catchups/tick/route.ts:37-38` (the whole advance, then `return`), `src/lib/retention.ts:178` (the sweep's steps), and every `reportSwallowed` inside an `after()` (`email-queue.ts:526, :550`, `link-preview.ts:346, :392, :398`, `collection-intake.ts:75, :152`)

- Expected: `report-error.ts:21-22` — *"the one line that keeps the guard and removes the hiding"*; `retention/sweep/route.ts:23-35` — the reason `maxDuration` was raised was so a failure "reaches the `reportSwallowed` that every other failure here goes through".

- Known-related: T2a-14 (nothing records that the tick ran — this is the other way the tick can be silent), T5-10/T8b-18 (the reporter's quota). Not in the baseline.
